Private Beta

You're in. Let's get PADE on your phone.

We're onboarding beta testers one at a time while PADE finishes App Store and Google Play review. Follow the steps below — you can be up and running today.

01 Tell us how to find you

You're already approved for the beta — we just need one thing from you to add you to the list.

  • On iPhone, send us your Apple ID email. Check Settings → your name at the top of the screen if you're not sure — it's often different from your everyday email.
  • On Android, send us the Gmail account signed into your Play Store app.

By submitting, you agree to be added to PADE's private beta list and contacted about your invite.

Prefer email? Send your name, phone type, and Apple ID or Google Play email to accounts@tfo.work.

02 Install the beta

Once we add you, follow the steps for your phone below.

iPhone
via Apple TestFlight
  1. Install TestFlightSearch "TestFlight" in the App Store and install it — it's Apple's free, official app for testing betas.
  2. Wait for your inviteOnce we add your Apple ID, Apple emails you an invite (check spam). Usually within 1 business day.
  3. Accept & installOpen the invite email on your iPhone, tap "View in TestFlight," then "Accept," then "Install."
  4. Open PADELaunch PADE from your home screen, just like any other app.
  5. Allow permissionsTap Allow for Camera (to scan and show QR codes at checkout) and Allow for Notifications (so you know the moment a sale comes in).
  6. Set up your gigCreate your account, add your first gig, and you're live.
Android
via Google Play beta
  1. Send your Google Play emailSame as Step 1 — it's the Gmail account signed into your Play Store app (Play Store → your profile icon, top right).
  2. Get your opt-in linkOnce we add you to the closed testing group, we'll email you a link to join.
  3. Become a testerOpen that link on your Android phone, tap "Become a tester," then tap the Play Store link it gives you.
  4. Install PADEInstall PADE from the Play Store, just like any other app.
  5. Allow permissionsAllow Camera (for QR code payments) and Notifications (for sale alerts) when prompted.
  6. Set up your gigCreate your account, add your first gig, and you're live.

Heads up — TestFlight builds expire after 90 days, and Google Play beta spots are limited while we're in private testing. We'll re-invite you as needed, and email you the moment PADE goes fully public.

Questions

Why can't I just download PADE from the App Store?

We're finishing Apple and Google's review process. Until then, we're onboarding testers by hand through TestFlight and Google Play's closed testing — it's the same app, just an earlier door in.

Is my Apple ID or Google Play email safe with you?

We only use it to add you as a tester in App Store Connect or Google Play Console. We never see your password — Apple and Google handle sign-in entirely on their end.

What if I don't hear back?

Email us at accounts@tfo.work and we'll get you sorted.
