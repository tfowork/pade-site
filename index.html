FourPay™

Most apps make invoicing
a whole separate thing.
Ours doesn't.

Sending an invoice has always meant stopping what you're doing, opening a different app, filling out a form, and hoping the client actually pays it. We thought that was ridiculous. So we put all four ways to get paid — right inside the cart, at checkout, every time.

Cash
Enter the amount tendered, and PADE instantly calculates change. One tap to record the sale — it's in your orders history before the customer walks out.
Card
Tap-to-pay through Stripe. No reader, no hardware — just hold the card to the phone. Payment confirmed, sale recorded, done. Works with any contactless card or device.
QR Code
PADE generates a unique payment link and QR code on the spot. The buyer scans it with their phone and pays on their own device — no card, no contact required.
Invoice
The differentiator
Enter a phone number or email and send a professional invoice in seconds — right from the cart, in person or remotely. Open invoices show as pending in your Orders page until paid. No separate app, no separate flow, no friction.
One cart. Four ways to close. Cash, card, QR, or invoice — all from the same checkout. For the first time, invoicing isn't a separate task. It's just another way to get paid.
