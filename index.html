FourPay™

Four Ways to Get Paid

Most apps make invoicing a whole thing. Not PADE. We think creating separate invoicing and POS workflows is ridiculous. So we put four ways to get paid right inside the cart. At checkout. Every time.

Cash
Simple. One tap records the sale. It's in your order history within seconds.
Card
Classic. Tap-to-pay right on your phone. No reader, no hardware needed. Payment confirmed, sale recorded, done.
QR Code
Ah, COVID. We do not miss you. But at least we got QR codes out of the deal. Customers scan and pay on their own devices. No card, no contact.
Invoice
kind of a big deal
Send professional invoices in seconds — right from your cart. Track open and paid orders. No separate app, no separate flow, no friction.
One cart. Four ways to pay. Cash, Card, QR, or Invoice — all from the same checkout. For the first time, invoicing isn't a separate task. It's just another way to get paid.
