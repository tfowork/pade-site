Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 8, 2026

PADE ("we," "our," or "us") is a product of The Future of Work. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the PADE mobile application and website (collectively, the "Service").

Website Launch Signup

If you submit your email address on trypade.com to be notified when PADE launches, we store that email address and the time it was submitted for the sole purpose of sending you launch and product update emails. We do not sell or share this information with third parties other than the email service provider we use to send those messages. You may unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email we send, or by contacting us at the address below.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly when you create an account, set up a gig, or process a transaction, including:

  • Name and email address
  • Business name and description
  • Transaction data (sales amounts, payment method type, customer records you enter)
  • Device information and usage data

We do not store full payment card numbers. Card processing is handled by Stripe. See Stripe's privacy policy at stripe.com/privacy.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the Service
  • To process payments through Stripe
  • To send you important account and service notifications
  • To improve and develop new features
  • To comply with legal obligations

Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Stripe — for payment processing
  • Service providers — who assist in operating the Service under confidentiality agreements
  • Law enforcement — when required by law or to protect our rights

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below.

Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes via email or through the app. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
